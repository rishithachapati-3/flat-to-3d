Methodology

Our Process

Five stages, one continuous loop — empathy always comes first.

Stage 1

Empathize

The starting point of every design-thinking process: what does the struggle actually feel like?

72% memorise formulas without understanding the mechanism
85% struggle specifically with 3D visualisation
68% cite abstract, non-visual lecturing as the main driver of backlogs
"I can solve it on paper — I don't know what it means in space."
Stage 2

Define

The problem statement that everything else in this project answers to.

Students need to interactively manipulate dynamic concepts — static 2D representation is the barrier.

Reframed focus: not student intelligence, but delivery architecture — shifting from memorising for exams to visualising to understand.

Stage 3

Ideate

Six features, one shared idea: let the student touch the concept, not just view it.

CORE INTERACTION

  • Multi-variable sliders — drag mass, speed, voltage, watch the model respond
  • Layer exploder — X-ray view into machinery, layer by layer
  • Concept-to-code — see the geometry behind an equation

SUPPORT & SAFETY

  • "What-If" AI assistant — instant answers, dynamically visualised
  • Faculty-guided customisation — models mapped to the syllabus
  • Error-free experimentation — no risk to physical lab equipment
Competitive Positioning

Why Not Existing Tools?

Good tools already exist. Here's the gap none of them close.

PhET / GeoGebra

  • Excellent, but single-subject
  • Not aligned to our syllabus
  • No instant doubt resolution

YouTube 3D Content

  • Passive, one-way viewing
  • No manipulation, no doubts answered
  • Scattered across creators

EduVerse 3D

  • One platform, every core subject
  • Faculty-aligned, AI-assisted
  • Built-in "What-If" doubt resolution

The real gap isn't better graphics — it's one consistent interface tied to what your own faculty is teaching that week.

Stage 4

Prototype

System architecture and interface — every control a student needs, nothing they don't.

Central 3D canvas, fully rotatable, with cross-sectional view
Live variable controls on the side
Explanation sidebar — theory linked live to the visual
Dark theme, royal blue & emerald green — easy on the eyes
Stage 4 — Applied

Magnetic Fields, Live

Formula on a board → a living field in space
Rotate 360°, adjust time, watch it change
